The PSD workflow

From layers
to your first video.

Keep the PSD's positions and transparency while checking each mouth and eye shape.

  1. 01

    Build the body

    Open Add Character → Create from PSD. Use the folder and layer eye icons to choose the body artwork.

  2. 02

    Assign mouth and eyes

    Choose a mouth or eye slot on the right, then check its layers in the center. Assigned layers are excluded from the body.

  3. 03

    Add speech and preview

    Create the character and place a WAV on its audio row. Play and adjust the analyzed mouth movement and blinking.

  4. 04

    Export transparent video

    Open File → Export Video and choose a format supported by your finishing editor.

Before you begin

Frequently asked questions

Find detailed steps in the illustrated user guide.

Open the guide →
Which PSD files are supported?

RGB, 8-bit supported raster layers. Several layers can form one body or shape. PSB, text, adjustments, special blending and effects are not fully reproduced.PSD support details

Do I need to keep the original PSD?

Yes. Projects reference the original. Use Collect Project when moving to another PC. External edits can update matching layers, but changes to IDs, hierarchy or positions require care.Keep and reload originals

Can I try it without drawing anything?

Yes. Free samples include artwork, speech and instructions. Start with the boy in sample 3 and choose its two full-body images.Choose a sample

How many images do I need?

Two aligned full-body images that differ only at the mouth are enough. Separate body and mouth parts also work. Add open/closed eye images for blinking.Prepare your media

Can I use Japanese or VOICEVOX speech?

Import WAV/MP3 from a speech synthesizer or a recording. Select Japanese / Other or English for each audio clip. Lip sync is estimated, so results vary with the voice and pronunciation.Analyze and adjust speech

What is a lip-sync generator?

It animates a character's mouth to match dialogue. LipSyncOne switches 2D mouth images frame by frame to make character clips for narrated explainers and commentary.

Does blinking work without audio?

Yes. Open and closed eye images create automatic blinking. Add an in-between image, change the interval and randomness, and set an active range per character.Blink settings

Why does an exported video look black?

Some players show transparent pixels as black. Import the clip into your editor over a background. Use an alpha-capable profile such as ProRes 4444, QuickTime Animation, FFV1 or VP9 Alpha.Transparent export

Do I need Internet access or an account?

Editing and analysis run locally, without uploading your images or audio. The analysis engine is bundled. Downloads, update checks and online help need an Internet connection.

How does the BOOTH version differ?

Core creation features are free. A BOOTH purchase supports development and includes purchase certification and access to supporter/early features. Check the actual package details on the BOOTH product page.
